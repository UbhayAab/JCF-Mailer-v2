What kind of campaign?

    

        

            Single email campaign
            
One message to one list. Write it, test it, send it now or schedule it.

        

        

            Multi email journey
            
A flowchart. Pick your base sheets, then build stages of emails with A/B splits,
               conditions on who opened or clicked, and loops for people who never respond.
               Everything is scheduled.
